# Screentime Strategy

Resources for teaching 11-year-old kids about screen time, dopamine, and self-regulation.

## Contents

- **screen-time.md** — Research-backed guide for parents: what the science says about screen time effects, how to explain it to kids, and strategies for teaching self-regulation
- **screen-time-video-discussion.md** — Discussion guide for watching "Solving Screen Addiction for Kids & Teens" (Media Stamped / Global Citizen) together. Pauses at chapter markers with conversation questions
- **screen-time-video-quiz.md** — Low-stakes comprehension quiz (13 questions) to check understanding after watching the video

## Approach

The goal is not to control or ban screens. It is to teach kids WHY excessive screen time is harmful so they develop self-regulation skills. They need to understand the reasoning, not just follow rules.
